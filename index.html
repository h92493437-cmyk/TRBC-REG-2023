Paste a list of fixes, one per line — Ref number, then a new balance and/or a claim status. This overwrites whatever's currently on file for that member, exactly like a single correction, just many at once. Formats accepted per line: Ref, then any of — a plain number for the new Upfront Plus balance, or 72=allocated / 72=owing (also accepts paid/unpaid, yes/no) for a claim status. Mix and match, separated by spaces or commas.

Example: 097 72=allocated 390 sets Ref 097's balance to R390 and Claim 72 to allocated. 103, 71=owing only changes Ref 103's Claim 71 status and leaves the balance untouched.

Add or replace a member

When a member passes away or defaults and someone else is taking over their slot, use "Replace" — it keeps the same REF number, but the incoming member starts fresh: balance R0 and every claim reset to owing. The old member's own history stays in the Activity Log (search by REF number to find it) but doesn't carry over onto the new person's slate. When it's a brand-new person joining the club, use "Add new member" to give them their own REF.
A new REF number is assigned automatically — the next one after the highest REF currently in the register.

Pay for someone else

Use one member's Upfront Plus balance to cover another member's deduction. The amount moves from the payer's balance to the payee's, then the payee is checked for auto catch-up.

Active claims

Deduction amounts default to R110 unless you type a different amount here. "Apply to all" runs the deduction against every member right now: anyone with enough Upfront Plus balance is deducted and marked allocated; anyone short is left owing, without any balance taken from them. After that, any member who later makes a payment or receives a transfer that brings their balance up to the deduction amount is automatically caught up on that claim — no extra click needed.
